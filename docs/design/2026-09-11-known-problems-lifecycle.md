# Known-problem lifecycle (addendum to the known-problems pipeline spec)

Date: 2026-09-11. Status: rev 6 (defect-only cards and fixes, insight investigation threshold, floor 0.75; 2026-09-12). Companion to `2026-09-11-known-problems-pipeline.md`; this page is the rulebook Task 9 of the plan implements.

## Words

- **Problem**: one entry on a project's list (the spec's ticket). Its *definition* (name, control, what happened) is immutable after creation. *Presentation* (steps, quote) is generated by the pipeline.
- **Match**: a recording the cheap model linked to the problem. Counted once per recording per problem. Every match gets a monotonic **arrival number** from a per-problem sequence at insert time; deletion never reuses or lowers it.
- **Check**: the strong model re-reading one matched recording with screenshots. Outcome: `confirmed`, `refuted`, `inconclusive`, `unavailable`. A recording has at most one non-`unavailable` check per problem. Checks are **staged** on the batch until the batch finalizes; only finalized checks count anywhere.
- **Verified**: finalized checks with outcome `confirmed`. The only evidence a customer ever sees.
- **Cohort**: the set of finalized checks the bar is evaluated over. For a problem that has never been fixed, the cohort is all its finalized checks, across generations (an unpublished problem re-qualifies on retained checks). Once a generation is `resolved` (`fixed_at` set), the cohort for regression is only checks of recordings that occurred after `fixed_at`.
- **Incident**: the customer-facing record (an `error_groups` row) that exists only while a problem is published. It has a *generation* and a *fix substate*. Every investigation, fix attempt, PR, and card is stamped with its generation.
- **Evidence version**: a per-problem counter incremented exactly once per finalized batch, per deletion transaction, and per fold. Diagnoses and card copy are stamped with it.
- **Identity known**: at least one verified check in the cohort belongs to a recording with an identified user. Recomputed at every evaluation. Identity assignment or consolidation on a session emits a `recording_identity_changed` event that re-evaluates the bar for every problem the recording matches.

## States, fix substates, and investigation status

| State | Meaning | Customer sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tracking` | collecting matches and checks, has not passed the bar | nothing |
| `published` | passed the bar; has a live incident | an issue page; a card once a cause is found (eligibility below) and only for kind defect |
| `unpublished` | was published, later failed the bar; incident archived | nothing new; old links open, marked archived |
| `merged` | folded into a published problem; terminal | the target's card |
| `archived` | retired by a person; terminal and permanent | nothing |

Fix substate of a live incident: `none` → `fixing` → `pr_open` → `resolved`, with `fixing → none` (attempt failed) and `pr_open → none` (PR closed unmerged). `resolved` accepts no fix events. Fix attempts have ids; at most one **active** attempt per generation; a new attempt may start after a failure or closure. PR callbacks must match the attempt id and generation, else they are recorded as facts and ignored.

Investigation status of a live incident, independent of fix substate: `pending` → `done` (with `cause`, `coverage`, `evidence_version_used`) or `failed`. Reinvestigation never changes the fix substate.

Card eligibility: kind defect, state `published`, fix substate ≠ `resolved`, investigation status `done` with a cause whose coverage over the current verified observations is ≥ 50%. Fix eligibility (button, autonomy) has the same kind condition; an insight is never fixable. No time-based fallback. `resolved` incidents never render a card and are exempt from ordinary unpublication; only the regression transition evaluates them. The digest also lists, as one line without a card, incidents whose PR merged in the last seven days.

## The bar

Over the cohort:

- **passes** = confirmed ≥ 3, and ≥ 2 distinct identified users among confirmed when identity is known, and confirmed ÷ (confirmed + refuted + inconclusive) ≥ 0.40
- **fails** (evaluated only for `published` with fix substate ≠ `resolved`) = confirmed < 3, or (identity known and confirmed identified users < 2), or (counted ≥ 10 and confirmed ÷ counted < 0.25)
- otherwise **undecided**

## One activation transition

`activate_generation(problem, cohort)` is the only way an incident is created. It runs in one lease-guarded transaction and: archives any previous live incident; creates the incident with generation +1, fix substate `none`, investigation `pending`; links the cohort's verified observations; writes presentation from the cohort; stamps the evidence version; enqueues investigation for a defect, or for an insight whose confirmed identified users are at least `FRICTION_INSIGHT_INVESTIGATE_USERS` (default 5); invalidates any cached card. Initial publication, republication, and regression all call it.

## Scheduling

Four triggers, each idempotent per problem (one pending batch job per problem, keyed by `(problem, trigger_arrival_boundary)`):

1. **Arrival**: schedule when `matched_count ≥ 3` and `max_arrival_number − arrival_boundary ≥ 10` (or `arrival_boundary = 0`). `arrival_boundary` is set to `max_arrival_number` at batch selection. Because arrival numbers are monotonic, deletions cannot suppress future arrivals.
2. **Backlog**: after a batch finalizes, schedule the next batch immediately **only if** selectable work exists (unread matches that are not `unavailable`-and-not-yet-due). Otherwise schedule at the earliest `retry_at` or the next budget window, whichever applies.
3. **Retry**: `unavailable` checks get `retry_at` (1 h, 6 h, 24 h after each attempt; after the third, `permanent`). Retries are selected by the same batch selection, never as a separate path.
4. **Reconcile**: any transition that invalidates an in-flight batch (state change, generation change, fold, deletion) writes a `reconcile_needed` marker; the finalizer of the aborted batch, or the next scheduler tick, re-evaluates the bar over the finalized cohort and applies the resulting transition. Terminal states clear the marker without acting.

A batch = up to 10 selectable matches (30 for the very first batch when `matched_count > 50`), round-robin across identified users, oldest arrival first. The manifest (session ids) is written to the job before any model call. Each check is staged under the job's lease as it completes (a retried job skips already-staged sessions). The finalizer, in one lease-guarded transaction: re-reads state and generation and, if either changed, discards the staged checks' effect (they remain as data, `reconcile_needed` is set) and exits; otherwise promotes staged checks to finalized, increments the evidence version once, evaluates the bar, applies exactly one transition below, and schedules successors.

## Events, by state

### New recording matches

| tracking | published | unpublished | merged | archived |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| count +1; arrival trigger | count +1; arrival trigger (if fix substate is `resolved`, the recording joins the post-fix cohort) | count +1; arrival trigger | recorded against the target instead; the source is excluded from shortlists and nearest-ticket search | never matched; excluded from shortlists and nearest-ticket search so a replacement problem can be created |

### Batch finalizes (bar over the cohort)

| Result | tracking | published, substate ≠ resolved | published, substate resolved | unpublished |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| passes | duplicate check (below); if none, `activate_generation` | stay; link newly verified observations; presentation regenerated if a card has not been authored since; if `reinvestigate_needed` and evidence version > `evidence_version_used`, enqueue reinvestigation and clear the flag; fix workflow untouched; reinvestigation and an insight's first investigation follow the same kind and user-count rule as activation | this is a regression: `activate_generation` over the post-fix cohort; card returns with "Fix merged on <date>, seen again since"; old PR referenced | duplicate check; if none, `activate_generation` |
| fails | stay | → `unpublished`: incident archived; pending investigation and fix jobs for this generation cancelled; active fix attempt marked `superseded`; cached card invalidated; open PRs left open with outcome `superseded_diagnosis`; a later merge is recorded as a fact only | not evaluated | stay |
| undecided | stay | stay; link newly verified observations; reinvestigation rule as in `passes` | stay | stay |

### Duplicate check at publication

Before the finalizer transaction, against a snapshot: published problems with fix substate ≠ `resolved` and similarity ≥ 0.75 (`FRICTION_FOLD_MIN_SIMILARITY`, at most 10 candidates; every answer is recorded in `friction_gate_decisions`), ordered by similarity desc then id; each asked once "would one fix cover both"; first yes is the candidate target. Model calls happen outside any lock. The finalizer then takes the per-environment publication lock, re-reads the candidate set; if the chosen target is no longer published or a new candidate with higher similarity has appeared, the classification is re-run outside the lock and the finalizer retried (bounded to 3 tries, then publish without folding).

Fold, in the finalizer transaction: the source's matches **and their observation references** become matches of the target with fresh target arrival numbers (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` per recording; a recording already checked by the target keeps the target's check and is not re-checked); no source checks are copied; the target's arrival trigger is evaluated; the source → `merged` with `merged_into`; its pending batch cancelled. The target's customer-visible counts change only when its own batches verify the transferred recordings. If the target is later unpublished or archived, the source stays `merged`.

### Definition edited

Not allowed. A wrong name is fixed by archiving. Presentation is regenerated when the evidence version changes before a card is authored.

### Investigation finishes (published; stamped with generation and evidence version)

- Generation stale → recorded, ignored.
- Coverage recomputed against the current verified observations at render and at fix authorization; the stored `evidence_version_used` is the one attempted.
- Cause explains ≥ 50%: status `done`; the card becomes eligible for a defect; for a defect, if `friction_autonomy = auto_fix` and fewer than 5 fix PRs are open for the project and no active attempt: start a fix attempt (`none → fixing`). Otherwise button "Create fix PR". Insights show the cause on the dashboard only; no button, no auto-fix.
- Cause explains < 50%: status `done`, cause stored, card not eligible; `reinvestigate_needed` set; consumed only by a finalizer whose evidence version exceeds `evidence_version_used`.
- No cause or failed: status `failed`; card not eligible; `reinvestigate_needed` set the same way.

### Fix and PR events (published; carry generation and attempt id)

- "Create fix PR" click or auto-PR: only for a defect, and only reachable when a cause with ≥ 50% coverage exists (an insight returns not fixable); starts an attempt immediately. A second request while an attempt is active is ignored. Open-PR cap (5 per project) reached → auto-fix pauses; the manual button still works.
- Attempt starts: `none → fixing`. Attempt fails: `fixing → none`.
- Before opening a PR the fix job re-reads live publication, generation, and attempt; if stale, no PR; if the PR was created but the state check fails afterwards (ambiguous creation), the PR is recorded as an orphan artifact against the old generation and the attempt marked `superseded`.
- PR opened: `fixing → pr_open`. PR closed unmerged: `pr_open → none`. PR merged: `pr_open → resolved`, `fixed_at` set, card leaves the digest, matches keep counting into the post-fix cohort.
- Events after unpublish/archive: recorded as facts against their generation and attempt; no transitions.

### Recording deleted

Its matches, observation references, and checks are deleted (cascade); `reconcile_needed` set; in the same transaction the evidence version is incremented and, for `published` with fix substate ≠ `resolved`, the full `fails` predicate is evaluated over the cohort: fails → `unpublished` (same effects as a failing batch); otherwise cached card, quote, steps, and replay link are invalidated for regeneration. `resolved` incidents are untouched. Other states: counts recomputed only.

### Recording identity changed

Bar re-evaluated for every `published` problem the recording matches, as in deletion, in the same transaction.

### Person archives a problem

Any non-terminal state → `archived`, permanent. Incident archived; pending jobs cancelled; in-flight batches finalize as no-ops. Matches remain as data. Excluded from shortlists and nearest-ticket search from then on.

## Invariants a test can assert

1. At most one live incident per problem; `activate_generation` archives the previous one in the same transaction.
2. A card never shows a number, quote, step, or replay that is not derived from finalized `confirmed` checks against this problem's own definition at the current evidence version.
3. No investigation, fix, PR, or digest write takes effect with a stale generation or attempt; late events are facts only.
4. A recording has at most one non-`unavailable` finalized check per problem; `unavailable` retries at most three times.
5. `merged` and `archived` never transition again and never receive new matches.
6. Arrival numbers are monotonic per problem; `arrival_boundary` only moves forward and only at batch selection.
7. Folding moves matches and observation references, never checks; two published problems are never merged.
8. A "Why" is never shown, and a fix never auto-starts, when the cause explains fewer than half of the currently verified observations.
9. A `published`, non-`resolved` problem whose cohort fails the bar is unpublished in the same transaction that made it fail.
10. Every batch job produces exactly one of: a finalized transition, or a `reconcile_needed` marker consumed by a later evaluation.
11. `resolved` incidents render no card and are evaluated only by the regression transition over the post-fix cohort.
