Finds the bugs that impact real users. Opens a pull request when it can verify the fix.

Opslane watches errors and session recordings from your app and turns them into a short list of issues worth fixing. A factual impact filter and a repository inquiry decide which error issues deserve investigation. When Opslane can verify a fix, it opens a pull request. Otherwise, it records an insight or explains why a person must take over. A daily digest keeps you out of the dashboard.

## Who it is for

Teams who own a production web app and review their own pull requests. Opslane runs on your infrastructure, so you decide what it may touch and where the data goes.

## When to use it

- A bug reached users and you want the fix waiting for you rather than a ticket.
- Your error tracker holds thousands of events and five of them matter.
- Users rage-click or hit a dead button, and the console stays silent.
- You want a record of what ran and what passed before a change reaches review.

## How the docs are organized

**Get started** covers setup: self-hosting the stack, installing the SDK, and connecting GitHub. **How it works** explains the system for someone deciding whether to trust it, including how issues and fix pull requests work. **Configure** covers environments, API keys, and Slack notifications. **Trust and privacy** covers the data-flow model and replay masking.
